The ONS4 single-item anxiety score correlated strongly with the GAD-7 total score (Pearson $r = 0.509$, Spearman $\rho = 0.571$, $N = 13{,}761$ person-wave observations). Using a GAD-7 score of $\geq 10$ as the reference standard for moderate-to-severe anxiety (41.7\% of the sample), the single-item score achieved an area under the receiver operating characteristic curve of AUC~$= 0.802$ (95\% CI: 0.794--0.809).
